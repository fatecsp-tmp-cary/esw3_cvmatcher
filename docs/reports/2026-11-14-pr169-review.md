## Operational contract and limits

The sweep must be the first identity-lock operation in its transaction, or the
complete stripe set must already be held. It cannot safely expand an arbitrary
previously held subset. Commit/rollback promptly without network I/O. Sweeps
serialize identity writers during the database phase; no throughput/fairness
claim is made. This adds no unsupported source expiry mapping, stale-age/absence
policy, scheduler integration or release/deployment acceptance.
